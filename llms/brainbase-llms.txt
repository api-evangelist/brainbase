# Brainbase

> Brainbase is a modern, end-to-end brand-licensing and intellectual-property management platform used by global licensing businesses to run licensing, partnership and sponsorship programs — covering trademarks, patents, copyrights, licences, products and royalties. Founded 2016 in Los Angeles by Nate Cavanaugh, backed by Bessemer Venture Partners (Series A, 2020), acquired by PYXiS Software Group / Jonas Software (2022).
